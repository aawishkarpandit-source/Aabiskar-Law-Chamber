The Importance of Time Management

Prabesh: Awishkar, did you finish your homework? I still have a lot left to do, and    tomorrow is already near.

Awishkar: Yes, I finished mine in the evening. You should manage your time better instead of using your phone for hours.

Prabesh: I know, but I like playing games and watching videos after school. I think there is enough time to study later.

Awishkar: That is where you are wrong. If you keep delaying your work, you may have to rush everything at the last moment.

Prabesh: But making a timetable seems boring to me. I don't think I can follow the same schedule every day.

Awishkar: You don't need a strict timetable for every minute. Just make a simple plan and give enough time for homework, study, games, hobbies, and sleep.

Prabesh: Hmm, that actually makes sense. Maybe I waste too much time because I don't decide what I should do first.

Awishkar: Exactly! We should finish important schoolwork first and then enjoy our free time. That way, we can relax without worrying about unfinished homework.

Prabesh: I also stay up late sometimes because I start my homework too late. Then I feel sleepy in school the next morning.

Awishkar: I have done that too, and it makes studying harder. Getting enough sleep is also part of managing time properly.

Prabesh: So, time management is not only about studying all the time. It is about using our time properly for both work and fun.

Awishkar: Yes, that's right. If we use our time wisely, we can finish our work, enjoy our hobbies, and still get enough rest.

Prabesh: Thanks, Awishkar. I think I'll make a simple plan for tomorrow and try to follow it.

Awishkar: Good idea! Let's manage our time instead of wasting it, because time once lost cannot be brought back.
